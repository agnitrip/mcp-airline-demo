## Refunds (legacy — over-generous)

**All fares are fully refundable to the original form of payment, any time
before departure, no fee.** Applied miles are always redeposited immediately on
cancellation, with no 24-hour cutoff.

- Cancel online for an instant cash refund on any ticket, including the cheapest
  Economy Basic fares.
- No change fees on any fare, ever.

## Requesting a change

Cancel and rebook for free. Refunds post within one business day.
