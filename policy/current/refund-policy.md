# Fare Rules — Refunds & Changes

**Version:** 2026.3  **Status:** CURRENT — in force  **Last updated:** 2026-08-19
**Owner:** Revenue Management

This document is read at request time by the booking assistant. Changing it
changes what the assistant tells travelers — keep it accurate.

## Refunds by fare type

| Fare type            | Cash refund                         | Miles redeposit                     |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Economy Basic        | Non-refundable                      | Miles redeposited if canceled ≥24h before departure |
| Economy Flex         | Refundable to original form of pay  | Miles redeposited, no fee           |
| Premium / Business   | Refundable, no fee                  | Miles redeposited, no fee           |

- **Economy Basic is non-refundable for cash.** There is no "full refund
  anytime." Do not promise one.
- Miles applied to a booking are **redeposited** to the member's account if the
  trip is canceled at least **24 hours** before departure. Inside 24 hours,
  applied miles are forfeited on Economy Basic.
- A weather disruption declared by the airline waives change fees on any fare;
  it does **not** convert a non-refundable fare to cash-refundable.

## Requesting a change

Changes to Economy Basic incur a fare-difference charge. Flex and above change
free. All changes must be made through the reservations system of record.
