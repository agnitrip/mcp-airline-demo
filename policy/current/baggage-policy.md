# Baggage Rules

**Version:** 2026.3  **Status:** CURRENT — in force  **Last updated:** 2026-08-19
**Owner:** Airport Operations

## Carry-on

Every passenger: one carry-on bag plus one personal item, no charge.

## Checked bags by tier

| Tier      | Free checked bags | Each additional |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Member    | 0                 | $35             |
| Silver    | 1                 | $35             |
| Gold      | 2                 | $35             |
| Platinum  | 3                 | $35             |

- Overweight (>50 lb / 23 kg): $100 per bag.
- Gold and above may check bags free up to the tier limit on every fare type,
  including Economy Basic.
